# Hodge target: primitive multiple structures on W2 cannot realize the theta character

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** GREEN after two independent GRR reconstructions and correction of a transient Todd-term omission.  
**Millennium status:** scoped nonreduced-architecture obstruction only.

## 1. Geometry and target

Let \(C\) be a very general nonhyperelliptic genus-four curve,
\(A=J(C)\), and
\[
 i:S=W_2(C)\cong C^{(2)}\hookrightarrow A.
\]
Put
\[
 X=[C+p]\in NS(S),\qquad H=i^*[\Theta].
\]
The standard intersections are
\[
 X^2=1,\qquad XH=4,\qquad H^2=12,\qquad K_S=X+H. \tag{1.1}
\]
Set
\[
 D=3X-H.
\]
Then
\[
 D^2=-3,\qquad D K_S=-1. \tag{1.2}
\]
Poincare's formula and projection give
\[
 i_*1=\frac{\theta^2}{2},\qquad
 i_*X=\frac{\theta^3}{6},\qquad
 i_*H=\frac{\theta^3}{2},\qquad i_*D=0. \tag{1.3}
\]

For \(d=24k-1\), the desired quotient character is
\[
 q_k=12k\theta^2-8k\theta^3+(-24k^2+4k)\theta^4. \tag{1.4}
\]
Write
\[
 m=24k. \tag{1.5}
\]

## 2. Exact GRR constraints for a normally flat W2 multiple

Let \(E\in K_0(S)\) be the graded \(\mathcal O_S\)-class of a
normally flat multiple structure with reduction \(S\). For a retracted
finite-flat thickening this is simply \(E=r_*\mathcal O_Z\); for a
general nilpotent thickening one may take the sum of the canonical
nilradical-graded pieces when those pieces are locally free.

Grothendieck--Riemann--Roch says
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(i_*E)
 =i_*\bigl(\operatorname{ch}(E)\operatorname{td}(N_{S/A})^{-1}\bigr).
 \tag{2.1}
\]
Since \(T_A|_S\) is trivial,
\(c_1(N)=K_S\). Also
\[
 \operatorname{ch}(\mathcal O_S)
 =\frac{\theta^2}{2}-\frac{\theta^3}{3}+\frac{\theta^4}{8}, \tag{2.2}
\]
so the integrated degree-two part of
\(\operatorname{td}(N)^{-1}\) equals \(3\).

Comparing (2.1) with (1.4) gives:

\[
 \operatorname{rk}(E)=m, \tag{2.3}
\]
\[
 i_*c_1(E)=0, \tag{2.4}
\]
and
\[
 \int_S\left(
   \operatorname{ch}_2(E)-\frac12c_1(E)K_S
 \right)
 =m(1-m). \tag{2.5}
\]

### Todd-term firewall

The raw integrated degree-four target is
\[
 \int_A(q_k)_4=-m^2+4m=m(4-m). \tag{2.6}
\]
The rank-\(m\) Todd contribution is \(3m\). Subtracting it yields the
relative constraint \(m(1-m)\) in (2.5). Omitting this term produces
the wrong equation
\((m-1)((2m-1)u^2-u)=4(m-4)\). That transient formula is rejected.
Equation (2.5) is the independently reconstructed one.

For a very general \(C\),
\(NS(S)=\mathbb ZX\oplus\mathbb ZH\), and (1.3) shows that the kernel
of \(i_*:NS(S)\to H^6(A)\) is generated by \(D\). Hence (2.4) implies
\[
 c_1(E)=tD+\alpha,\qquad t\in\mathbb Z,\quad
 \alpha\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(S), \tag{2.7}
\]
where \(\alpha\) is invisible in rational cohomology. From (2.5),
\[
 c_2(E)=m(m-1)+\frac{3t^2-t}{2}. \tag{2.8}
\]
For \(k=1\), this is
\[
 \operatorname{rk}E=24,\qquad
 c_1(E)=tD+\alpha,\qquad
 c_2(E)=552+\frac{3t^2-t}{2}. \tag{2.9}
\]

These are constraints, not an existence theorem.

## 3. Primitive multiple schemes

A primitive multiple scheme \(Z\) of multiplicity \(m\) with smooth
reduction \(S\) is Cohen--Macaulay and locally embeddable in a smooth
variety of dimension \(\dim S+1\). Drézet's canonical filtration
\[
 S=S_1\subset S_2\subset\cdots\subset S_m=Z
\]
has an associated line bundle \(L\) on \(S\) and quotients
\[
 I_{S_j}/I_{S_{j+1}}\cong L^j.
\]
Therefore, independently of whether the gluing splits,
\[
 [\mathcal O_Z]=\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}[L^j]\quad\text{in }K_0(S). \tag{3.1}
\]

### Theorem 3.1

No primitive multiple scheme with reduction \(W_2(C)\) realizes the
target (1.4), for any \(k\ge1\).

### Proof

There is first a cheaper ambient-embedding contradiction. Because the
primitive multiple scheme is embedded in \(A\), its associated line
bundle is a quotient of the ambient conormal bundle:
\[
 N^*_{S/A}\twoheadrightarrow L. \tag{3.2}
\]
The kernel is a line bundle. Since
\[
 c_1(N^*)=-K_S,\qquad c_2(N^*)=6,
\]
we obtain
\[
 l(-K_S-l)=6. \tag{3.3}
\]
The degree-three target constraint below forces
\(l=uD+\alpha\), with \(\alpha\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(S)\). Using
\(D^2=-3\) and \(D K_S=-1\), equation (3.3) becomes
\[
 3u^2+u=6. \tag{3.4}
\]
Its discriminant is \(1+72=73\), not a square. Thus no embedded
primitive target exists already from the conormal quotient and the
degree-three constraint.

For an independent degree-four audit, put \(l=c_1(L)\) and
\[
 A_m=\frac{m(m-1)}2,\qquad
 B_m=\frac{m(m-1)(2m-1)}6.
\]
Equation (3.1) gives
\[
 c_1(E)=A_m l,\qquad
 \operatorname{ch}_2(E)=\frac{B_m}{2}l^2. \tag{3.5}
\]
Because \(A_m\ne0\), (2.4), the torsion-free Neron--Severi lattice,
and (1.3) imply
\[
 l=uD+\alpha,\qquad u\in\mathbb Z,\quad
 \alpha\in\operatorname{Pic}^0(S). \tag{3.6}
\]
Using (1.2), the left side of (2.5) becomes
\[
 \frac{B_m}{2}l^2-\frac{A_m}{2}lK_S
 =
 -\frac{m(m-1)}4\bigl((2m-1)u^2-u\bigr). \tag{3.7}
\]
The right side of (2.5) is
\[
 m(1-m)=-m(m-1). \tag{3.8}
\]
Cancelling the nonzero factor yields the exact Diophantine gate
\[
 (2m-1)u^2-u=4. \tag{3.9}
\]

Here \(m=24k\ge24\). If \(u=0\), the left side is zero. If \(u\ge1\),
it is at least \(2m-2\ge46\). If \(u\le-1\), it is at least
\(2m\ge48\). No integer solution exists. \(\square\)

Thus every primitive/curvilinear multiple structure on \(W_2\) is
excluded, including nonsplit primitive gluings.

## 4. The surviving nonprimitive sector

For an arbitrary thickening with nilradical \(J\), the canonical
graded algebra is
\[
 \operatorname{gr}\mathcal O_Z
 =\bigoplus_{j\ge0}J^j/J^{j+1}. \tag{4.1}
\]
At a normally flat point its generic fiber is a finite local quotient
of a two-variable polynomial algebra because \(S\subset A\) has
codimension two. The primitive theorem handles exactly the
embedding-dimension-one (curvilinear) sector. A survivor must therefore
have embedding dimension two generically somewhere, or fail normal
flatness/local freeness in a way whose torsion corrections pay the
GRR debt.

For locally free graded pieces, their aggregate class \(E_{\rm gr}\)
must satisfy (2.3)--(2.8). This is the exact punctual-Hilbert-scheme
target. No seed, splitting, or retraction may be assumed beyond the
hypotheses used to define \(E_{\rm gr}\).

## 5. Claimant / critic / rebuilder

**Claimant.** A nonsplit high-multiplicity rope on \(W_2\) might
supply the negative Euler characteristic while keeping the correct
lower Chern terms.

**Critic.** Primitive gluing does not alter the \(K\)-class geometric
series (3.1). The conormal quotient gives the nonsquare discriminant (3.4), while GRR independently reduces every such attempt to (3.9), which has no
integer solution at multiplicity \(24k\).

**Rebuilder.** The remaining \(W_2\) lane is sharply localized:
nonprimitive, embedding-dimension-two punctual algebras with aggregate
rank \(24k\), determinant in the \(D\)-direction, second Chern class
(2.8), and all sixteen weak-semiregularity characteristic classes
closed. Canonical monomial, square-zero, \(I\)-adic, and primitive
architectures are already excluded in companion notes.

## 6. Provenance and scope

- Jean--Marc Drézet, *Primitive multiple schemes*,
  arXiv:2004.04921 / European Journal of Mathematics 7 (2021), for
  the definition, canonical filtration, associated line bundle, and
  quotients \(L^j\).
- Standard Macdonald/Poincare formulas for \(C^{(2)}=W_2(C)\).
- Standard GRR for the regular embedding \(S\hookrightarrow A\).

The very-general hypothesis is used for the stated Neron--Severi
kernel. This note does not rule out arbitrary nonprimitive multiple
structures, does not construct a semiregular object, and does not
prove the Hodge conjecture.
