## 4. Conditional resultant-effectivity test

Locally lift the degree-six minimal generator B. The binary resultant of the
degree-five and degree-six forms is invariant under changing that lift by
B to B+A times a linear form. It therefore defines a global section of
\[
 (\det V)^{30}A^{-6}B^{-5}, \tag{11}
\]
with class
\[
 \Delta=30v-6a-5b=9a-245v+5tD. \tag{12}
\]
If this resultant is not identically zero, Delta is effective. At (8),
\[
 \Delta=1538X-243H,\qquad
 \Delta H=3236,\qquad \Delta X=566. \tag{13}
\]
Thus even this elementary numerical positivity test does not exclude the
survivor. If the two forms have a common factor everywhere, the section is
identically zero and effectivity cannot be inferred.

## 5. The everywhere-ghost proposal and its fatal gap

A second fiberwise Betti table has a canceling ghost pair in degree seven:
generator ranks 1,1,4 in degrees 5,6,7 and syzygy ranks 1,4 in degrees 7,8.
Let K be the degree-seven syzygy line. Locally its relation is
\[
 q_2A+u_1B=0, \tag{14}
\]
where q_2 is quadratic and u_1 linear. No degree-six syzygy implies q_2 and
u_1 are coprime; fiberwise A and B have a common quartic.

The tempting argument regarded U=KB^{-1} as a subline of V and Q=KA^{-1} as a
subline of Sym^2(V), then applied stability. The second subbundle is not
canonical. Changing the local degree-six lift by B to B+A times ell changes
\[
 q_2\longmapsto q_2-u_1\ell. \tag{15}
\]
Only u_1:U to V globalizes as a subbundle map. The quadratic globalizes only
modulo u_1V:
\[
 Q\overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}
 \operatorname{Sym}^2V/(U\cdot V)
 \simeq (V/U)^2. \tag{16}
\]
Accordingly
\[
 c_1(Q)+2c_1(U)=2c_1(V) \tag{17}
\]
is tautological, not contradictory, and the claimed degree bound on Q is
unavailable. The common quartic does not split (16).

This is the same local-to-global error isolated by the Tor-K firewall. The
proposed everywhere-ghost no-go is therefore **withdrawn** unless an additional
compatible lift or splitting theorem is proved.

Claim + counterexample mechanism + best salvage:

* Failed claim: every later minimal generator or coefficient in a relative
  syzygy embeds in the corresponding symmetric power.
* Counterexample mechanism: positive-degree upper-triangular changes of minimal
  bases; (15) is the concrete instance. The P1 dual-number example in the
  firewall is a fully explicit realization.
* Best salvage: use the quotient (16), or prove the geometric embedding supplies
  a canonical splitting. No such splitting is known.
