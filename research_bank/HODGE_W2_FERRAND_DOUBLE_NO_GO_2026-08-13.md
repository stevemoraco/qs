# Ferrand doubles on \(W_2\) cannot realize the theta-secant character

**Date:** 2026-08-13  
**Status:** GREEN exact ribbon no-go under the stated generic Néron–Severi hypothesis. Not a Hodge-conjecture result.

Let \(C\) be a generic nonhyperelliptic genus-four curve,
\[
X=J(C),\qquad S=W_2(C)\cong C^{(2)}\subset X.
\]
Write
\[
\eta=[p+C],\qquad \theta=x|_S.
\]
Macdonald's intersection formulas give
\[
\eta^2=1,\quad
\eta\theta=4,\quad
\theta^2=12,\quad
K_S=\theta+\eta.
\]
Put
\[
D=\theta-3\eta.
\]
Then
\[
D\theta=0,\qquad
D^2=-3,\qquad
DK_S=1.
\]

## 1. Ferrand double setup

Suppose \(S'\) is a Cohen–Macaulay double structure on \(S\) with a square-zero exact sequence
\[
0\longrightarrow L
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_{S'}
 \longrightarrow\mathcal O_S
 \longrightarrow0.
\]
For a multiplicity-two CM rope on the smooth surface \(S\), the nilradical \(L\) is a line bundle, and an embedded Ferrand double requires a surjection
\[
N^*_{S/X}\twoheadrightarrow L.
\]
Let \(M\) be its line-bundle kernel:
\[
0\longrightarrow M
 \longrightarrow N^*_{S/X}
 \longrightarrow L
 \longrightarrow0.
\]

## 2. The target forces \(c_1(L)\) into the primitive kernel

GRR gives
\[
\operatorname{ch}(i_*L)
 =i_*\left(1+\left(c_1(L)-\frac{K_S}{2}\right)+\cdots\right).
\]
Since
\[
\operatorname{ch}(\mathcal O_S)
 =\frac{x^2}{2}-\frac{x^3}{3}+\frac{x^4}{8},
\]
matching the secant target ratio
\[
\frac{\operatorname{ch}_3}{\operatorname{ch}_2}=-\frac23
\]
for the double forces
\[
i_*c_1(L)=0\in H^6(X,\mathbf Q).
\]

For generic \(C\),
\[
\operatorname{NS}(S)
 =\mathbf Z\theta\oplus\mathbf Z\eta,
\]
and the kernel of
\[
i_*:\operatorname{NS}(S)\to H^6(X,\mathbf Z)
\]
is generated by \(D=\theta-3\eta\). Thus
\[
c_1(L)=tD
\]
up to a \(\operatorname{Pic}^0(S)\)-twist, for some \(t\in\mathbf Z\).

## 3. The conormal Chern equation is impossible

Because \(T_X|_S\) is trivial,
\[
c(N_{S/X})=c(T_S)^{-1}.
\]
The known invariants of \(C^{(2)}\) give
\[
c_2(N_{S/X})=6.
\]
From
\[
c(N^*)=c(M)c(L)
\]
and
\[
c_1(N^*)=-K_S,
\]
we obtain
\[
c_2(N^*)=(-K_S-c_1(L))c_1(L).
\]
Substituting \(c_1(L)=tD\) yields
\[
6=-t(K_SD)-t^2D^2=-t+3t^2.
\]
Therefore \(t\) would have to satisfy
\[
3t^2-t-6=0.
\]
Its discriminant is
\[
1+72=73,
\]
which is not a square. There is no integer solution.

## Theorem

Under the generic Néron–Severi hypothesis above, no embedded Cohen–Macaulay Ferrand double supported on \(W_2(C)\) has the Chern-character ratio required by the theta-secant target.

## Claim / critic / salvage

**Claim.** A double structure on \(W_2\) might alter the top Chern character while preserving the favorable codimension-two CM local algebra.

**Critic.** The degree-three target forces the nilpotent line into the primitive class \(tD\), while the conormal quotient forces \(3t^2-t=6\). The nonsquare discriminant kills every embedded double before the degree-four coefficient is considered.

**Salvage.** Higher ropes, non-locally-free nilradicals, mixed supports, and general nonsplit Hilbert–Burch resolutions remain open. The theorem applies only to multiplicity exactly two with line-bundle nilradical on generic \(W_2\).

No Lean verification or Millennium claim is made.
