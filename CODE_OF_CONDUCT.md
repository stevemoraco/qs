# Code of Conduct

This project follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, or publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
