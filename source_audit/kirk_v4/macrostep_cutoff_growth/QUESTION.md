# Macrostep cutoff-growth quantifier audit

Theorem 4.30 defines a support exponent from the one-step supplier constants and then chooses the localization degree. This audit asks whether those supplier constants are uniform in the localization degree, or whether the source proves a cross-degree growth bound strong enough to justify that order of choice.

The logical repair is one of:

1. a localization-degree-independent supplier bound;
2. an explicit estimate `log_2 K_*(D) <= a D + b` with `a < 1`; or
3. a direct choice of one degree `D` satisfying `D-3 > log_2 K_*(D) + 2 + epsilon`.
