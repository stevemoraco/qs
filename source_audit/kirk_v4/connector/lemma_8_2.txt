   A scale-labelled source forest is generated recursively as follows. A source derivative is a labelled leaf.
At one conditional integration, connected cumulant differentiation joins a nonempty partition of the
incoming leaves and decorated subtrees to one new vertex labelled by that integration scale. Terms
transported without joining retain their existing components. At the terminal matching logarithm, the
remaining components are joined by the matching-scale connected cumulants. The ordering of children is
fixed by the least source label, so isomorphic terms are not counted more than once.
