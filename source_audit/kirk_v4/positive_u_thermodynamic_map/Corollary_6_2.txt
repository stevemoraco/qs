Lemma 8.38 (No low-dimensional gauge-invariant vector). For every compact simple G there is no
nonzero local, reflection-compatible, gauge-invariant vector source of engineering dimension at most four,
modulo vacuum terms and total derivatives.
Proof. A connection mass term is not gauge invariant. At dimension two, Tr dρ(Fµν ) = 0 by semisimplicity.
At dimension three, traces of dρ(DF ) reduce to derivatives of Tr dρ(F ) and vanish. A dimension-four
gauge-invariant vector cannot be formed from two curvature two-forms using only the metric while leaving
one free index; an epsilon tensor would have the wrong reflection parity. Total derivatives are excluded
by the stated quotient.
