lattice gauge theory, the signed-hypercubic and reflection-even gauge-invariant scalar jet quotient has no

Bianchi descendants are already removed in the quotient. Thus the reflection-even scalar sector jumps

Lemma 5.23 (No reflection-even rank-two curvature jet of dimension five). In the local gauge-invariant
rank-two curvature-jet quotient relevant to the stress/displacement source, the reflection-even engineering-
dimension-five component is zero.
Proof. A dimension-five curvature bilinear has the schematic Lorentz-index content F DF , hence five
tensor indices before contractions. To leave exactly two free rank-two indices one would have to contract

complement after dimension four therefore begins at dimension six.

D      Ordinary periodic torons, seams, and wrapping templates
The finite-volume model used throughout is the ordinary periodic product space GE(Λ) with periodic
gauge transformations. It is a single, explicit lattice model; no twisted boundary condition and no sum
over nontrivial principal-bundle sectors is part of the construction. Since the target spacetime R4 is
contractible, this restriction is compatible with the theorem’s local continuum scope.
  Define the compact set of ordinary periodic flat holonomies

                          TG := {(h1 , h2 , h3 , h4 ) ∈ G4 : [hµ , hν ] = e for all µ, ν}.

Lemma D.1 (Tree-gauge reduction of ordinary periodic flat fields). Every flat configuration in the
ordinary periodic link model is gauge equivalent to a seam representative whose interior links are the
identity and whose four fundamental seam holonomies form an element of TG . Conversely every element
of TG defines such a flat periodic configuration. The remaining gauge action is simultaneous conjugation
of the four holonomies.

Proof. Choose a maximal lattice tree in a fundamental periodic cell and gauge its links to the identity.
Flatness makes the holonomy of a path depend only on its homotopy class. The four links closing the
fundamental cycles therefore supply holonomies hµ . The commutator loop of two fundamental cycles
is a product of plaquette boundaries, so flatness gives [hµ , hν ] = e. Reversing this construction gives
a flat seam representative from every commuting four-tuple. A gauge transformation at the tree root
conjugates all four holonomies simultaneously, and all other gauge freedom has already been fixed.
