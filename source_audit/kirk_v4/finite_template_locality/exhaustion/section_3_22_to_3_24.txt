Lemma 3.24 (The exact forty-nine-path block satisfies DAS). The Wilson Hessian, local Haar and
coarea endpoint two-jets, the optical projector, and the compatible-core geometry satisfy Definition 3.23,
uniformly in finite volume and weak depth. The collar RG may be chosen once so that κG (RG ) < 1/8.
Proof. The Wilson plaquette Hessian and Feynman grounding term have fixed finite range. The forty-nine-
path constraint and each chart/coarea endpoint live on one fixed block template. The coefficient projectors
are the complementary projections of the complete two-jet extraction, and the stagewise conditionals are
chain-rule factors of one normalized joint law. The actual constrained precision is Qopt = T ∗ Hf T . The
analytic-strip proof of Corollary 4.10 applies to T , Hf , and Qopt itself, so both precision and covariance
have uniform exponential kernel rows. Restricting that row to two cores separated by RG gives (1).
Merging the finitely many cores that violate the separation condition preserves the action charge and
leaves one compatible family. Enlarge RG until the displayed smallness holds.
