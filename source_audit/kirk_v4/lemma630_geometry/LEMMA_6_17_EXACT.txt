Lemma 6.17 (Carrier incidence and original-support locality). Let p be a live pivot on an active path γ.
With all other active path links fixed,
                                        Wγ = Bp Xp±1 Cp ,                                           (51)
where Bp , Cp are independent of Xp . If a connected physical activity with original reported support Y
meets k separated live pivots, then
                                          τ (Y ) ≥ cp R(k − 1).                                     (52)
Anchor reconstruction on an inactive path cannot alter the activity’s live pivot incidence.
Proof. Equation (51) is the ordered product split before and after p. The second statement follows by
joining the k pivot buffers inside the connected reported support. Every inactive-path coordinate is a
function only of links on that inactive path; because no live pivot lies there, its substitution cannot create
dependence on a live pivot.
