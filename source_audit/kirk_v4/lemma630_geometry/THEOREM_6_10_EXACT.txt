       P Haar factor for each live pivot. Fubini and exact ownership split the physical interaction into

distinguished insertion.
   The one-pivot oscillation is precisely the complete one-pivot ledger proved above; normalization changes
only an U -independent scalar. For the multipivot row, every activity is reported in its original connected
fine-link support. Split it by the three species in Proposition 6.8. A reference factor meeting separated
pivots has diam A ≥ cp R(|IP (A)| − 1); a regular or irregular connected factor has |A| ≥ cp R(|IP (A)| − 1).
Reserve respectively mref − a′ref , areg − a′reg , and airr − a′irr in the three input rows. After multiplying
by the pivot charge eλ|IP (A)| , the reserved exponent absorbs that charge for sufficiently large finite R
and yields exactly the three terms of ∆R (u). Thus no diameter estimate is used to pay a spanning-tree
weight. Coordinate substitutions on inactive paths are independent of all live pivots and cannot create
additional pivot incidence. Physical-source marks of order at most two change only Cλ ; the independent
pivot derivatives through order 2sG are already included in the three norms in (HT5).
   Finally, on an active path no anchor is reconstructed: all path links remain original variables. Splitting
the ordered path product immediately gives (49). Haar invariance therefore annihilates the Haar part of
every nontrivial carrier at an uncovered pivot under the same integral in which the product reference was
