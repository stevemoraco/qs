We construct four-dimensional pure G Yang–Mills theory as a zero-spacing, infinite-volume limit of reflection-positive Wilson lattice measures. The Wilson plaquette function is normalized at the identity to the background quadratic form used to define the coupling u = g2 . A weighted forty-nine-path Karcher block, complete sixteen-alias normal section, charged chart- complement partition, and permanent rooted domain produce a uniform scale-two map on a centered invariant graph, u(2L) = u(L) + 11 CA(G) log 2 24π2 u(L)2 + OG(u(L)3 ). At the physical handoff, a group-dependent product Sobolev order 2sG > dG/2 gives a Peter–Weyl Fourier–Wiener landing for the exact one-pivot and multipivot densities. Separated Haar pivots contract nonvacuum compact flux, optical covariance contracts normal-field stretches, and all remaining vertices retain their support charge. A single marked replica–BKAR forest then gives a boundary-to-center two-root connector and one physical clustering rate. General invariant-jet transport, displacement and stress Ward identities, finite dimension-four mixing, and a dimension-six rotation regulator yield a tempered, reflection-positive, translation-continuous, O(4)-covariant Schwinger family. Osterwalder–Schrader reconstruction gives a positive-energy Poincaré-covariant local theory with unique vacuum and a strictly positive Hamiltonian gap. Endpoint-safe curvature-source transport produces a nonzero local vector with positive spectral mass in a finite interval above the gap. The theorem concerns the local gauge-invariant curvature-jet observable theory.
