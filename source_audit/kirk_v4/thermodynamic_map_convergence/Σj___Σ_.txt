Lemma 5.22 (No reflection-even scalar of engineering dimension five). In four-dimensional pure fixed-G
lattice gauge theory, the signed-hypercubic and reflection-even gauge-invariant scalar jet quotient has no
engineering-dimension-five component.
Proof. A nonvacuum dimension-five scalar would have to contain two curvature factors and one covariant
difference, hence five coordinate indices before contraction. The invariant tensors of the signed hypercubic
group are generated by even-rank Kronecker contractions and the rank-four alternating tensor. They
cannot contract an odd total of five indices to a scalar without a preferred vector. Total differences and
Bianchi descendants are already removed in the quotient. Thus the reflection-even scalar sector jumps
from the extracted dimension-four Yang–Mills density to dimension at least six.

Lemma 5.23 (No reflection-even rank-two curvature jet of dimension five). In the local gauge-invariant
rank-two curvature-jet quotient relevant to the stress/displacement source, the reflection-even engineering-
dimension-five component is zero.
Proof. A dimension-five curvature bilinear has the schematic Lorentz-index content F DF , hence five
tensor indices before contractions. To leave exactly two free rank-two indices one would have to contract
an odd set of three indices. The signed-hypercubic invariant tensors available in the reflection-even
sector are products of Kronecker deltas and pairs of Levi–Civita tensors, all of even rank. A single
Levi–Civita tensor is reflection odd. Thus no reflection-even constant coefficient tensor can perform the
required contraction. Terms in which the derivative is moved onto the whole bilinear are total differences,
while Bianchi and equation-of-motion descendants are already quotiented. The next possible rank-two
complement after dimension four therefore begins at dimension six.
