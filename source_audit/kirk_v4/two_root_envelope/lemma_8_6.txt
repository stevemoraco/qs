5017: Proof. For one conditional integration, differentiation of the normalized logarithm is the joint-cumulant

5019: Bruno formula: every block of the partition is one incoming connected component and the outer cumulant
5020: joins those blocks at the current scale. Fixing the least-label order makes this representation unique.

5022: logarithm is the final induction step. The weak rooted margin pays every nonroot weak vertex by σ < 1,

5025: summable independently of m, so dominated convergence gives the last assertion.
