copies of g. The result is a finite sum of complete scale-labelled packet trees such that:
    (i) the two source leaves have one unique lowest common ancestor;
   (ii) each g mark is owned exactly once by a trajectory coordinate, a conditional logarithm, a source
        return, the terminal weak logarithm, or the compact handoff logarithm;
  (iii) every covariance edge, hard-core derivative, field factorial, regulator allocation and normalization
        contact belongs to one complete one-step packet before multiscale composition;
  (iv) the coefficients are exactly those of the Banach-valued Faà di Bruno formula.
No infinite-depth passage is asserted in this lemma.
Proof. Apply the joint-cumulant derivative identity to every exact normalized weak conditional and, on
a crossing cell, to the exact finite macrobridge and joint compact–optical logarithm. Then apply the
Banach-valued Faà di Bruno formula to the finite composition. This assertion is coefficientwise and uses
only finite-volume differentiability; the uniform compact marked bound is supplied later by Theorem A.20.
The one-step summed replica–BKAR theorem controls each weak packet, Lemma A.4 controls zero,
one and two g marks, and finite source transport controls the external roots. Uniqueness of the lowest
common ancestor is the ordinary tree property.
Lemma A.12 (Top marked ancestor and complete-packet shell). In a packet tree from Lemma A.11,
contract every maximal descendant subtree carrying no distinguished label into its exact parent-normalized
decoration, and suppress every maximal unary chain into its exact ordered transfer product. The distin-
guished labels are the two curvature roots and the zero, one or two active g marks. They have a unique
lowest common ancestor v⊤ ; let j be its conditional scale. Call v⊤ the top marked ancestor.

contracted side decoration with its canonical reported support. Decompose these whole coefficients by their
full reported-support shell k using a scalar translation- and reflection-invariant selector. The selected
class is therefore a complete BRS/reflection packet with every denominator and normalization contact
still inside it.

Proof. Contraction of an unlabelled descendant is its exact conditional value, so it retains its canonical
reported support. Suppression of a unary chain is the exact ordered transfer product. After these
operations, a tree with at most four distinguished leaves has only a fixed number of essential branching
edges, while all arbitrarily long unary portions are already contained in the parity transfer product. The
unique lowest common ancestor of all labels is an ordinary tree invariant. If a g derivative lands on a
coarser conditional map, its mark is attached at that map and therefore moves v⊤ to that scale. Hence
no marked vertex can occur above v⊤ .
   The shell selector is applied only after the full packet at v⊤ has been collected. A remote unmarked
side decoration is not cut from its parent and is not asked to carry an external Taylor factor. If exact
localization turns its contribution into a smaller-hull contact, that term is already in the finite contact block
and is not a shell-k packet. Otherwise its canonical reported support remains part of the whole packet.
When that support spans 2k−j scale-j blocks, the strict rooted-support norm and the weak connector
theorem give (208), including covariance, hard-core and mixed alternatives. The exact Loc/collect identity
of Theorem 5.8 ensures that this dichotomy is made before the stable packet is declared.
