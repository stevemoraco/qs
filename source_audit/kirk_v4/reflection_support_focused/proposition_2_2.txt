1.6     Scope
The theorem concerns the local gauge-invariant curvature-jet observable sector for each fixed compact
connected simple G. It does not assert a bound uniform over groups, a simultaneous large-rank limit,
control of every extended Wilson-line or generalized-symmetry sector, or uniqueness of the continuum
limit outside the directed construction. Quotient groups are included through their actual compact link
group. The construction uses only the ordinary periodic finite-volume sector and retains all flat toron
backgrounds occurring in that sector; no claim about a sum over twisted boundary conditions is made or
needed.

2     Fixed-group Wilson geometry and Karcher blocking
Let Λ be a finite periodic four-dimensional lattice and use the reflection-positive Wilson measure of
Proposition 1.2. For each oriented retained link, use the straight two-link path with weight 7/8 and the
forty-eight signed-corner staples with weight 1/384 each. On a fixed strongly convex identity tube, define
the retained link as their weighted Karcher mean in the bi-invariant metric.
Lemma 2.1 (Fixed-group convex Karcher and holomorphic atlas). There are radii 0 < r0,G < r1,G
such that the squared-distance functions, product, inverse, exponential, logarithm, and weighted Karcher
critical point extend holomorphically to the corresponding local complexification tube. The Karcher Hessian
is bounded below there by a positive constant depending only on G. All derivatives through arbitrary
prescribed finite order are bounded on the smaller tube.
Proof. A compact Lie group with a bi-invariant analytic metric has positive injectivity and strong-convexity
radii. The sectional curvature is bounded because G is compact, and the Hessian comparison theorem
gives a positive lower Hessian bound on a sufficiently small ball. In a faithful matrix representation
the local complexification is an open neighborhood in the complexified group; multiplication, inversion,
exponential, and the principal logarithm are holomorphic there. The analytic implicit-function theorem
applied to the Karcher critical equation gives the holomorphic mean. Cauchy estimates on nested compact
tubes give every fixed derivative order; standard compact Lie-group analytic facts are summarized in
[15].
Proposition 2.2 (Exact block covariance and local submersion). The retained map is gauge covariant,
orientation covariant, signed-hypercubic covariant, and aligned-reflection covariant. Its linearization at
the flat connection has full retained rank. On one fixed weak tube it admits a finite-volume analytic
normal section whose matrix kernel and first three marked derivatives are exponentially local, uniformly
in volume.
Proof. Every path has the same endpoints, and the weighted squared-distance functional is invariant
under simultaneous left and right multiplication. Uniqueness of the Karcher minimizer gives gauge and
orientation covariance. The signed-corner path family is invariant under the lattice symmetry group.
At the flat point, the exact Fourier symbol is the color-free spacetime symbol tensored with Ig and has
acoustic value 2I ⊗ Ig and a uniformly gapped normal frame. The full-alias section and normal-fiber
contraction are proved in the later normal-chart chapters.
