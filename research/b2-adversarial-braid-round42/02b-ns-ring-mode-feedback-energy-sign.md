## 1. Setting

Let

`U(r)=V(r)e_theta+W(r)e_z`

be a steady divergence-free Euler vortex column, and let `u` be a real perturbation obtained from an exponentially growing helical normal mode with

`Re(lambda)>0.`

Let

`R_0=<u tensor u>_(theta,z)`

be the zero-helical-phase Reynolds tensor. Because `U` and `grad U` are independent of the helical variables, only `R_0` contributes to the integrated perturbation-energy exchange.

The exact linearized Euler energy identity is

`(1/2)d/dt ||u||_2^2
 =-int R_0:grad U
 =Re(lambda)||u||_2^2>0.`

## 2. The reaction force on the mean

In the phase-averaged nonlinear mean equation, the perturbation contributes the force

`f_mean=-P div R_0.`

Since `U` is divergence-free and `P` is the orthogonal projection onto divergence-free fields,

`int U dot f_mean
 =-int U dot P div R_0`

`=-int U dot div R_0`

`= int R_0:grad U.`

Using the growing-mode energy identity,

`boxed:
int U dot f_mean
 =-Re(lambda)||u||_2^2<0.}`

Thus the wave's positive energy-growth rate is exactly the negative work of the Reynolds reaction on the parent:

`boxed:
wave energy gain + parent work by feedback =0.}`

This is the quadratic energy-transfer law behind the instability.

## 3. Consequence for recursive self-reproduction

The prior bank correctly proves that

`P div R_0 !=0`.

The present sign theorem sharpens its interpretation. Nonzero mean feedback alone does not show that a narrower next parent is amplified. Its instantaneous work against the existing parent is strictly negative.

A recursive ring cascade would therefore need a more refined redistribution theorem:

1. although total work on `U` is negative, the feedback creates a localized correction `delta U`;

4. viscosity and nonzero double-helical channels remain lower order.

None follows from nonvanishing of `P div R_0`.
